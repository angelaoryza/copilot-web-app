9.1 What deploying means

Deploying means copying your app to a server so it runs for everyone at a public address. A host is the service that does this, in three steps:

A host… On Streamlit Community Cloud
Stores your files Takes a copy of your GitHub repository (Module 8)
Builds your app Installs the packages listed in requirements.txt
Serves your app Runs app.py and answers visitors at your-app.streamlit.app

Your app builds each page from live data and what visitors type, so it needs a host that runs Python, such as Streamlit Community Cloud.

Hands-on

9.2 Deploy on Streamlit Community Cloud

Do it
1

Go to share.streamlit.io and sign in with GitHub.

2

Click Create app, then Yup, I have an app.

3

Choose your Repository, the Branch main, and the file path app.py.

4

Optional: choose an App URL, for example idx-snapshot-yourname.

5

Click Advanced settings. In Secrets, paste this one line with your real key between the quotes: SECTORS_API_KEY = "your-key-here". Leave the Python version as it is.

6

Click Save, then Deploy. The build log appears on the right; the first build takes two or three minutes.

What to look for

your app opens in the browser; BBCA shows the company, chart and calculator, as on your laptop. If the log ends in red lines, call a mentor.

9.3 Why .env does not travel

The host copies your files from GitHub, and .gitignore kept .env off GitHub. So the live app gets the key from Secrets, a private settings box on the host. sectors.py looks for SECTORS_API_KEY in .env first and in Secrets second, so the same code works in both places.

On your laptop When deployed
Where the key lives .env Secrets in the app’s settings
Format SECTORS_API_KEY=your-key-here SECTORS_API_KEY = "your-key-here" (quotes needed)
Hands-on

9.4 Open it on your phone

Do it

Send your app’s link to yourself, open it on your phone, and try a ticker. Then send it to the person next to you.

Every push now updates the live app by itself. The flow for any change is: change → test locally → commit → Sync Changes → wait a minute → refresh the live page.
